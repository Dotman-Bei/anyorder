The order book of this batch

Clearing sorts by limit price and never reads arrival order. It has no Ctx or caller effect; the only identity it reads is each order's recorded owner, for the final tie-breaks, at most 1 base unit per order.

How clearing reads the bookorderAt()
  • Sorted bylimit price, best first
  • Never readarrival order
  • Final tie-breakowner, then owner's sequence

Batch clearing and claims

Every filled order trades at one uniform price. Orders at the marginal price share what is left pro rata by quantity.

Rounding dust is at most 1 wei per order, and the contract keeps it.

One sandwich, pool against batch

  1. 1. The front-run

    The attacker buys {{rec:pool.attackerTokens}} just before the victim. In a pool that raises the victim's price.

  2. 2. The victim's buy

    In the pool the victim gets {{rec:pool.victimSandwiched}}, not {{rec:pool.victimAlone}}. In the batch: {{rec:batch.victimTokens}} for {{rec:batch.victimPaid}} either way.

  3. 3. The back-run

    The attacker sells straight after. The pool pays a profit; in the batch both legs clear at your price.

Where the batch guarantee stops

The victim gets the same fill as without the attacker under condition C: equal leg sizes, the buy limit above every other limit and the sell limit below every other.

One-sided, the attacker's buy moves the price to {{rec:oneSided.price}}: the victim pays {{rec:oneSided.victimPaid}} and the attacker pays {{rec:oneSided.attackerPaid}} at that price.
