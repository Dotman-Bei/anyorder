Trade as one of five built-in traders

Pick a trader, then send a buy or a sell with a limit price. Your order waits in the open batch until it closes.

Each trader starts with 1,000 TKN and 100 ETH, half of each deposited. Anvil dev accounts. Test funds, no real value.

The order book of this batch

Clearing sorts by limit price and never reads arrival order. It has no Ctx or caller effect; the only identity it reads is each order's recorded owner, for the final tie-breaks, at most 1 base unit per order.

How clearing reads the bookorderAt()

Batch clearing and claims

Every filled order trades at one uniform price. Orders at the marginal price share what is left pro rata by quantity.

Rounding dust is at most 1 wei per order, and the contract keeps it.

One sandwich, pool against batch

  1. 1. The front-run

    The attacker buys {{rec:pool.attackerTokens}} first; in a pool that lifts the victim's price.

  2. 2. The victim's buy

    In the pool the victim gets {{rec:pool.victimSandwiched}}, not {{rec:pool.victimAlone}}. The batch fills {{rec:batch.victimTokens}} for {{rec:batch.victimPaid}} either way.

  3. 3. The back-run

    The attacker sells straight after. The pool pays a profit; the batch clears both legs at one price.
